# Levana Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

Levana is a free, open-source Hebrew calendar app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

**Levana does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the developer never receives any of it.

## Information that stays on your device

Levana works offline. To provide its features it uses the following, only on your device:

- **Location:** to calculate zmanim (halachic times) and candle-lighting times for where you are. You can pick a city from a list instead and never grant location access. If you choose "Use GPS", your device's location services find your position, and Android's built-in geocoder looks up the nearby city name. On most devices both are provided by Google and are covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Levana keeps only the result, on your device.
- **Contacts:** only if you add Hebrew birthdays. Levana reads your contact list so you can pick a person, and saves the Hebrew birthday into that contact on your device.
- **Calendars:** only if you turn on System calendars. Levana reads your device's calendars to display their events alongside the Hebrew calendar.
- **Notifications:** to remind you of candle lighting, holidays, fasts, the Omer, birthdays and your own events.
- **Your settings and events:** your cities, preferences, personal events and yahrzeits are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything Levana stored.

## Information sent off your device

Levana sends nothing about you to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service.

The only network connection Levana itself makes is an **update check**, and only if you installed the app from its GitHub releases page rather than from Google Play. That check asks GitHub's public API for the latest version number. The request contains no personal information, but like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. It is covered by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Copies installed from Google Play never make this request.

## Exporting your events

If you export your events, Levana writes a JSON or CSV file to the location you choose. What you do with that file is up to you.

## Children

Levana does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. The full history is available in the app's public source repository.
