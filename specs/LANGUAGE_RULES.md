# English and transliteration

- **English UI:** use English for features, controls and explanations: **Halachic times**, **Torah reading**, **Cities**. Use transliteration for individual halachic times and Jewish names (holidays, months, readings). **Candle lighting** is the English exception.
- **Hebrew UI:** use Hebrew; omit duplicate English/transliterated subtitles. English detailed time rows may include Hebrew subtitles. Never translate user-entered names or notes.
- Use Israeli/Sephardi **t**, **ch**, **tz**, **sh**, and sentence case; preserve proper names and acronyms. Use the exact [spellings](SPELLINGS.md), not alternative transliterations. Compact tiles: **Netz**, **Shkia**, **Tzeit**.

Full EN/HE glossary and formatting policy: [TERMINOLOGY.md](TERMINOLOGY.md).
