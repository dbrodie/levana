Levana is a Hebrew calendar for Orthodox Jewish users in Israel and the Diaspora. No ads, no data collection.

• Hebrew and Gregorian month views, plus an Agenda with search
• Holidays, fasts, parsha, Omer and molad, for Israel or the Diaspora
• Zmanim, candle lighting and havdalah for your location or any city
• Yahrzeits, Hebrew birthdays and other events by their Hebrew date
• Your phone's calendar events alongside the Jewish dates
• Reminders for candle lighting, holidays, fasts, the Omer and your events
• Widgets, a Quick Settings tile and an icon showing today's Hebrew date
• Hebrew or English, light or dark

Free and open source: github.com/dbrodie/levana
This project uses AI-generated code and assets.
