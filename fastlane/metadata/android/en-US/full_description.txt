Levana is a Hebrew calendar for Orthodox Jewish users in Israel and the Diaspora. It works completely offline, has no ads, and collects no data.

📅 HEBREW & GREGORIAN CALENDAR
• Month view in Hebrew or Gregorian months, with Hebrew dates on every day
• Holidays, fasts and the weekly parsha
• Israel and Diaspora schedules, including second-day Yom Tov
• Optional modern Israeli holidays
• Jump to any Hebrew or Gregorian date

🕯️ ZMANIM
• Netz, shkia, tzeit and the full list of halachic times for your location
• Sof zman Shema (GRA and Magen Avraham) and Tefillah, chatzot, mincha gedola and ketana, plag hamincha
• Candle-lighting and havdalah times, with a configurable candle-lighting offset
• Choose which times appear in the day view
• Pick your city from a built-in list, or use your phone's location

🗓️ YAHRZEITS, BIRTHDAYS & PERSONAL EVENTS
• Add Hebrew birthdays to your contacts and see them on the calendar
• Track yahrzeits and other recurring events by their Hebrew date
• Adar in leap years handled the way your community does it, with settings for the annual and first-year yahrzeit and for dates like 30 Cheshvan and 30 Kislev
• Export and import your events

🔔 REMINDERS
• Candle lighting, holidays, fasts, Sefirat HaOmer, birthdays and your own events

✨ MADE FOR YOU
• Full Hebrew interface (עברית) and English
• Holiday-themed colors
• Shows events from your phone's calendars

Levana is free and open source: github.com/dbrodie/levana
